### Bored stiff ...:

Informal way to say extremely bored.

> Context usage : 
>  - bored stiff at work
>  - bored stiff in class
>  - bored stiff during a meeting
>  - bored stiff by a movie
>  - bored stiff waiting for something
>    

Example : 
 - I was bored stiff at presentation.
 - I am bored stiff by this game.
 - She bored stiff waiting for him.
