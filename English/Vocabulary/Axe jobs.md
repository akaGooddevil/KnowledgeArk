### Axe jobs:

means to eliminate jobs, axe means to remove or get rid of something.

### Common Patterns
- **axe jobs**
- **axe hundreds of jobs**
- **axe thousands of jobs**
- **axe 500 jobs**
- **jobs are being axed**
- **jobs could be axed**

Examples : 
- Ai axed lot of jobs.
- Company will axe 10 jobs next week.
