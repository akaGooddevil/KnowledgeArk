### Dispose of ... :

means to get rid of something , especially something you no longer need.
> dispose of = get rid of

### Common Collocations
- dispose of **waste**
- dispose of **rubbish/garbage**
- dispose of **unwanted items**
- dispose of **old furniture**
- dispose of **documents**
- dispose of **chemicals**
- dispose of **household waste**
- dispose of **something safely/properly**


Example : 
- Please dispose of your rubbish properly.
- You need to dispose of old broken furniture.

**Dispose of** can also mean **to deal with or settle something**, especially in formal contexts.

> The court **disposed of the case** quickly.

Here, it doesn't mean literally throwing something away. It means **to deal with and bring something to an end**.
