### Make changes:

to change or modify something.

We usually use **make changes to something**.

Examples:

- We need to make some changes to the schedule.
- The new management will make fundamental changes to the business.

### Formal alternative

Use **implement changes** in formal contexts, such as business or academic writing.

### Related expressions

- **bring about change**: to cause a transformation
- **implement change**: to put a modification or new plan into practice
