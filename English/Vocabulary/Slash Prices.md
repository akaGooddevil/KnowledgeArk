### Slash Prices:

to reduce prices by a large amount and usually suddenly, in simpler word, to cut the price.

### Common Patterns
- **slash prices**
- **slash the price**
- **slash prices by 20%**
- **slash prices dramatically**
- **slash prices to attract customers**
- **slash the cost of something**

Example : 
-  Stores will slash the price by 50% on every season change.
- They slashed the price to attract customers.

### Related Collocations

You can also use **slash** with:
- **slash costs**
- **slash spending**
- **slash expenses**
- **slash the budget**
- **slash taxes**
