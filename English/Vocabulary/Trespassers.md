### Trespassers:

someone who enters into someones land without any permissions.
### Common Collocations

- **keep out trespassers**
- **prevent trespassers**
- **warn trespassers**
- **catch trespassers**
- **remove trespassers**
- **unauthorized trespassers**
- **private property**

Example :
- They hired a guard to keep out the trespassers.
- Trespassers will be prosecuted.
- He tried to trespass on old man land.
